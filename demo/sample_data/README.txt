BraTS Dual-Domain Brain Tumor Segmentation — Sample Data Instructions

For testing, upload BraTS 2023 Glioma dataset volumes:
1. Primary scan: upload a T2-weighted MRI file (*-t2w.nii or *-t2w.nii.gz).
2. Ground truth (optional): upload the matching segmentation file (*-seg.nii.gz) to calculate Dice Similarity Coefficients (DSC) and show the ground truth overlay.

Ensure the files are skull-stripped, co-registered, and maintain standard BraTS dimensions (240x240 in the axial plane).
